## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-22

The first public release for the Miyoo Mini Plus.

### Added
- **Automatic updates over Wi-Fi.** Startide looks for a newer release a few seconds after it
  starts, tells you when one is out, and installs it from the Start menu when you say so.
  Every file is checked before anything is replaced; your session, saves and samples stay.
- Three pages, each a pixel-art painting that answers the sound: **Grain Storm** (a valley at
  dusk, dawn or under the moon), **Cloud Cathedral** (a torii gate at blue hour, in morning
  mist or by moonlight) and **Starloom** (aurora in green, violet or blue).
- Granular engine with roam, pitch jumps and reverse grains; the **cloud reverb** (1.5 s to a
  minute, wind, ensemble, shimmer, freeze); **tape** wow, flutter, hiss and saturation.
- **PLAY mode**: the face buttons play the current chord in key, with six hold styles
  (SOFT, HOLD, SWELL, ARP, STRUM, GRAIN).
- **AUTO DJ**: chapters with moods, gentle key changes, an evening arc from dusk to dawn,
  kept moments as new samples, and a sleep timer.
- A twelve-sound sample library, your own WAVs as grain sources, tuned to the music's key.
- **TAPE view**, recording the last 20 s as a sample, four save slots, and a session that
  picks up where you left off.
- **Wi-Fi MIDI** (RTP-MIDI) with MIDI channel, AUTOPLAY and FIT TO KEY.
