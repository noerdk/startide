# Code of conduct

Startide is a calm instrument, and this is meant to be a calm place.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to handhelds or to making music.
- Assume good intent; disagree about ideas, never about people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep issues on topic and free of spam or promotion.

Comments, issues or users that don't follow this may be edited, closed or blocked. To report
something, use GitHub's *Report* option on the comment or contact the maintainer.
